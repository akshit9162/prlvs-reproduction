  1. The pipeline is not the ceiling

    Knowing the human scores reaches 0.729 through it.

  2. The reward carries no human signal

    Maximising it directly scores no better than random.

  3. Training changes nothing measurable

    Against the untrained network: p = 0.44 faithful, 0.15 repaired.

A fix, half working

A held-out importance model joins the reward. Moves of ±1 or ±5 from random starts cap even direct maximisation at 0.064; the agent learns less.

Every unstated decision, written down

The paper cannot be implemented as printed. PROVENANCE.md records each choice: from the paper, from the reference reproduction, or decided here, and where Eq. 4 and Eq. 13 are ambiguous.

At K = 10 the literal reward is a product of at most 1e-10; the repaired variant computes it in the log domain.
